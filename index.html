What is Pokémon? • Pocket Monsters: A media franchise created in Japan in 1996 by Satoshi Tajiri. • Core Concept: Humans catch, train, and battle fictional creatures called Pokémon. • Expansions: Grown into video games, an animated series, a massive trading card game (TCG), and global merchandise. Popular Games and Media • Nintendo Switch: Titles like Pokémon Scarlet, Pokémon Violet, and Pokémon Shining Pearl let players explore open regions and catch hundreds of species. • Pokémon Unite: A multiplayer online battle arena featuring periodic character additions like Jolteon and Flareon. • New Releases: Creative spin-offs and expansion packs continue to launch regularly for Nintendo consoles. Trading Cards and Merchandise • TCG Sets: Special expansions and new card collections drop throughout the year, including themed seasonal sets. • Collectibles: Official plush toys, apparel, and special collaborative merchandise lines are sold via the Pokemon Center.
